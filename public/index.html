How to play
  • Pull back like a slingshot: pull down for distance, pull right to aim left (and left to aim right), and let go to fling a koala off the Rock. A gold ring means it will hit.

  • A Moses who reaches the horde parts it, scattering koalas. Stop him first.
  • Land on gum leaves to charge the CUDDLE STAMPEDE. Land on red flags to recruit stragglers.
  • Thrown koalas run back to the horde. Lose every koala and the comeback is over.
